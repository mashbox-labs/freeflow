# Contributing to Free Flow

Thank you for your interest. Bug reports, reproduction steps and pull requests are welcome.

## Pull requests

Free Flow is source available under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0: free for personal, school and nonprofit use; it may not be sold. MASHBOX Labs also ships it through the App Store and as a notarized Mac download. We merge outside code only under a contributor licence agreement (CLA) that lets us distribute the combined work under that licence and in those channels.

That agreement does not exist yet. Until it does, we do not merge outside code. You can still open a pull request to show a fix or an idea; we will read it, and we may fix the same thing ourselves. We will say so in the pull request.

## Reporting bugs

Open an issue with:

- the device and OS version (for example iPhone 15, iOS 18.1, or MacBook Air, macOS 15.1);
- the speech model you had selected (Settings > Models);
- what you did, what happened, and what you expected;
- for the keyboard, whether Allow Full Access is on.

Please do not attach audio of other people.
